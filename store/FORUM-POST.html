Torn gives you two choices. Light mode, and dark mode. That's it.

For a game I've spent a frankly embarrassing number of hours staring at, that felt like a crime. So I fixed it.

Theme Hub for Torn is a browser extension that gives Torn 23 colour themes. Click the icon in your toolbar, pick one, and it applies instantly. No page reload, no settings maze, no nonsense.

Stuff I actually cared about getting right

You can read it. Half the "dark themes" out there are dark grey text on slightly darker grey. Every theme here was checked so text keeps real contrast against its background, in both Torn's light and dark mode, on every panel I could get my hands on.

Colours that mean something stay meaningful. Money is still green. Your energy, nerve, happy and life bars keep their colours, because that's how you tell them apart at a glance. Jail, hospital and travel keep their tints by default so you always know where you are (there's a toggle if you want those themed too).

It plays nice with TornTools. I run it myself. They sit side by side without stepping on each other.

The wiki is left alone. It's a different site with its own look, and theming it made it look like a crime scene. So it doesn't.

"Is this allowed?"

Yes. I read the scripting rules before I wrote a single line, and built it to stay well inside them.

  • It doesn't talk to Torn, or anything else. There is no network code in it at all.
  • It only recolours the page you're already looking at. It doesn't read other pages, doesn't touch game actions, and doesn't go anywhere near CAPTCHAs.
  • Your theme choice is saved in your own browser and never leaves it.

Don't take my word for it. The full source is on GitHub, nothing minified, nothing hidden. Or open your browser's dev tools, go to the Network tab, and watch it do absolutely nothing.

It's not perfect (yet)

Torn is enormous. There are pages I haven't set foot on in years, and some of them are going to look a bit off. If you find one:

  • Reply here with the page and a screenshot.
  • If you're feeling generous, right-click the broken bit, hit Inspect, and paste the HTML. That turns a "hmm" into a five-minute fix.

I'll fix it. Probably within a day. Possibly while I should be training. Updates install automatically through the store, so you don't have to do anything.

Theme requests are welcome too. If your faction colours deserve their own theme, make your case.
